# Mockup library licences

Every mockup in this folder was rendered for Digital Canvas with Blender (Cycles) from procedural
scenes in `tools/mockups/` (geometry, materials and lighting written in Python; no photos, HDRIs,
textures or third-party models). The images and their UV/shade/tint passes are dedicated to the
public domain under **CC0 1.0** (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

No photographs are used. If a photo-based mockup is added later, list its author, licence (CC0 or
public domain only) and source URL here and in its `credit` field.
