Privacy policy

Digital Canvas is a free, open-source design editor (source code). Last updated: 27 October 2026.

What we store

When you sign in with Google, our small backend stores: your Google account id, email and name, the time and number of your sign-ins, and an encrypted refresh token for Google Drive so the app can reach your designs from any device after you connect once. Nothing else.

Your designs

Your designs are files in your own Google Drive (a “Digital Canvas” folder). They go directly between your browser and Google Drive; they never pass through or get stored on our servers. The app uses Google's drive.file permission: it can only see files it created or that you open with it, not the rest of your Drive.

Use of Google user data

Digital Canvas's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell data, show ads, or use your data for anything except running the editor for you.

Cookies and analytics

No advertising or analytics trackers. The browser keeps your sign-in session and a local cache of design assets.

Deleting your data

Sign out and remove “Digital Canvas” at Google account › Third-party access to revoke Drive access. Ask us to delete your account record by opening an issue or emailing ppanth977@gmail.com. Your Drive files stay yours.
