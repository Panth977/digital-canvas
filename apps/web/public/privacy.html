Privacy policy

Digital Canvas is a free, open-source design editor (source code). Last updated: 28 October 2026.

What we store

When you sign in with Google, our small backend stores: your Google account id, email and name, the time and number of your sign-ins, and an encrypted refresh token for Google Drive so the app can reach your designs from any device after you connect once. Nothing else.

Your designs

Your designs are files in your own Google Drive (a “Digital Canvas” folder). They go directly between your browser and Google Drive; they are never stored on our servers. The app uses only Google's drive.file permission: it can see the files it created and the files you choose yourself with Google's file picker (Uploads › Google Drive › Choose from Google Drive) or open with it, never the rest of your Drive. A file you pick is copied once into your “Digital Canvas” folder so you can use it in your designs.

AI connector (optional). If you connect an AI assistant (for example Claude) to Digital Canvas through our MCP connector at https://digital-canvas-p977.web.app/mcp, the assistant's requests are handled by our server function: it reads and writes the designs and assets you ask it to, in your Drive, and renders previews and exports. Design data is only held in memory while a request runs; exports are saved to your Drive. We keep the connector's access tokens (so the assistant stays connected) and short-lived export job records, nothing else. You can disconnect at any time from your assistant's settings. If you use the local connector (npx @digital-canvas/mcp), everything runs on your own computer.

Use of Google user data

Digital Canvas's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell data, show ads, or use your data for anything except running the editor for you.

Cookies and analytics

No advertising or analytics trackers. The browser keeps your sign-in session and a local cache of design assets.

Deleting your data

Sign out and remove “Digital Canvas” at Google account › Third-party access to revoke Drive access. Ask us to delete your account record by opening an issue or emailing ppanth977@gmail.com. Your Drive files stay yours.
