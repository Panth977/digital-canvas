Privacy policy

Digital Canvas is a free, open-source design editor (source code). Last updated: 28 October 2026.

What we store

When you sign in with Google, our small backend stores: your Google account id, email and name, the time and number of your sign-ins, and an encrypted refresh token for Google Drive so the app can reach your designs from any device after you connect once. Nothing else.

Your designs

Your designs are files in your own Google Drive (a “Digital Canvas” folder). They go directly between your browser and Google Drive; they are never stored on our servers. The app uses Google's drive.file permission: it can only see files it created or that you open with it. If you choose Connect your whole Google Drive, it also gets read-only access (drive.readonly) so the Uploads panel can list your files; nothing is copied unless you add it to a design.

AI connector (optional). You can connect an AI assistant (for example Claude) to Digital Canvas through our MCP connector at https://digital-canvas-p977.web.app/mcp. Where its work happens depends on your setting in File › Settings › AI and devices:

  • On your devices (the default). Your designs are opened, rendered and exported by Digital Canvas running on a device of yours where you turned on Let AI use this device; it talks to your Google Drive directly. Our server only relays: it passes each request from the assistant to your device and the answer back, through short-lived records in our database (the request, and the answer, which can include preview pictures of your design). They are deleted as soon as the answer is delivered, and automatically within an hour at the latest. For each device we keep its name (for example “Ada's Mac – Chrome”), its type, whether you allowed AI on it and when it was last online, so you and the assistant can pick it; you can remove a device in the same panel.
  • In the cloud (available to some accounts only, if they choose it). The assistant's requests are handled by our server function: it reads and writes the designs and assets you ask it to, in your Drive, and renders previews and exports. Design data is only held in memory while a request runs; exports are saved to your Drive. Long video exports keep a short-lived job record.

In both cases we keep the connector's access tokens (so the assistant stays connected) and your processing choice, nothing else. You can disconnect at any time from your assistant's settings. If you use the local connector (npx @digital-canvas/mcp), everything runs on your own computer.

Use of Google user data

Digital Canvas's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell data, show ads, or use your data for anything except running the editor for you.

Cookies and analytics

No advertising or analytics trackers. The browser keeps your sign-in session and a local cache of design assets.

Deleting your data

Sign out and remove “Digital Canvas” at Google account › Third-party access to revoke Drive access. Ask us to delete your account record by opening an issue or emailing ppanth977@gmail.com. Your Drive files stay yours.
